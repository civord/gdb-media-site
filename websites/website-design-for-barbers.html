Common Questions

How much does a barbershop website cost?

One fixed price up front, GST included, no lock-in — a one-chair studio and a six-chair shop with walk-ins need different sites, so you get a quote built around yours. Get a free quote and you'll know your exact number before any work starts.

Can you connect my Booksy, Fresha or Square booking?

Yes. We wire the "Book Now" button straight to whatever system you already use, so clients book themselves in — even at midnight — while you stay mid-fade. If you don't have one yet, we'll set up a simple booking-request flow.

Will I show up for "barber near me"?

"Near me" searches lean on your Google Business Profile — which we'll help you set up or optimise — plus a fast, mobile-first site with local signals and your real cuts on display. That combination is what gets a scroller to stop and book.

Should I put my prices on the site?

Yes — transparent pricing builds trust and filters out the tyre-kickers. A clear, easy-to-update price list means no awkward surprises at the register and more of the clients you actually want.

Can you show off my best cuts?

The gallery is the pitch. We build a fast gallery that keeps your fades and beard work sharp on every phone, because in barbering nobody reads about a skin fade — they look at one.
