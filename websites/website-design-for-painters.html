Website Design for Painters — Australia-wide

You are not selling paint. You are selling preparation.

Two quotes for the same house, one four thousand dollars apart, and the customer cannot see why. The difference is sanding, filling, priming and two proper coats instead of one thin one — all of it invisible by the time the job is done. If your site does not explain that, you are quoting against someone who skips it.

Why It Matters

The cheap quote wins until you explain why it's cheap.

Prep Is Invisible And Expensive

It is most of your labour and none of what the customer sees. Photograph it, describe it, put it in the quote as a line item. Painters who explain prep stop losing jobs to painters who do not do it.

Repaints Are A Different Sale

A new build is a builder's contract. A twenty-year-old weatherboard is a homeowner worrying about lead paint, rotten sills and whether you will be gone in a week. Same brush, entirely different conversation.

Strata Work Is Steady Money

Body corporates repaint on cycles, pay reliably and rarely change painters once they trust one. They also search differently, want insurance and compliance detail, and almost never appear on a painter's website.

Common Questions

Straight answers, no fine print.

How much does a painting website cost in Australia?

One fixed number, quoted before any work starts and GST inclusive. A solo residential painter needs less than a business running strata contracts and commercial crews, so the scope drives the price. No hourly billing, no lock-in.

How do I stop being undercut on price?

By making the invisible visible. A page that shows sanding, filling, priming and the number of coats, with photos of each, changes what the customer thinks they are comparing. Without it, every quote looks like the same job at different prices.

Are before-and-after photos worth it for painting?

Only if the before is genuinely bad and the after is shot from the same angle in the same light. Half-hearted pairs read as staged. One honest set of a peeling weatherboard house turned around is worth twenty finished-room photos.

Should I chase strata and body corporate work on the site?

If you want repeat revenue, yes. Strata managers search for painters with the right insurance and a track record on multi-unit sites, and they hire on cycles rather than once. Very few painters have a page aimed at them.

Do you build separate pages for interior and exterior?

Yes, and for roof painting too. They are different price points, different seasons and different searches. A single painting services page competes for all of them and wins none.
