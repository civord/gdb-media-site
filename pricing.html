The Part That Matters

The site is yours to keep.

There is a common deal around at the moment: a website for very little money, paid monthly, forever. What is not always spelled out is who owns it. In most of those arrangements the agency keeps the domain, the files and the content — so the month you stop paying, the site goes dark and you start again from nothing.

We do not do that. You own the domain, the files and the content from day one. If you want to move it to another host, or hand it to someone else in two years, you can, and we will help you do it. The build is paid once.

That is also why we would rather quote honestly than win on price. A $200 website exists, and what you get for $200 is a template with your logo on it and no plan for how anyone finds it.

Common Questions

Straight answers, no fine print.

How much does a website cost in Australia?

For a small business or trade business, a real custom site generally lands between $800 and $3,000. Below about $500 you are usually buying a template with your logo dropped in. Above $5,000 you are usually paying for an account manager and an office. Our packages run from $799 to $2,990, and the price is fixed before work starts.

Do I own the website?

Yes. You own the domain, the files and the content, and you can take the whole thing elsewhere whenever you like. A lot of cheap monthly website deals keep ownership with the agency, which means the day you stop paying the site disappears. We do not work that way.

Do I have to pay monthly?

No. The build is a one-off fixed price. Hosting and maintenance are optional and separate, and you can host it yourself if you would rather. There is no lock-in contract on anything.

What is not included in the price?

The domain name, which is roughly $20 a year, and professional photography if you want it. Everything else needed to launch is in the package price. If something falls outside the quote we tell you before we do it, not after.

Can I start small and upgrade later?

Yes. Plenty of trades start on Starter to get something live, then add pages as the work comes in. You pay the difference between the tiers, not the full price again.

How long does a website take to build?

Starter is usually about a week. Business is two to three weeks. Complete is three to four weeks. The main delay is almost always waiting on photos and content from your end, so the sooner that arrives the sooner you are live.

Do you offer payment plans?

Fifty per cent to start and the balance when the site goes live, on every package. For the Complete package we can split it across three payments if that suits your cash flow better.

Affordable Web Design

Our small business website packages sit in between on purpose. Enough budget to write real copy, build real service pages and set the search fundamentals up properly — without the overhead of an agency charging for an account manager you will speak to twice.
