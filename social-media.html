Social Media for Trades — Australia-wide

Your work is the content. You already made it.

Every job you finish is a before and an after. Most tradies have hundreds of them sitting in a camera roll doing absolutely nothing. Social media for a trade business is mostly a system for getting those out the door consistently — which makes it a habit problem, not a creative one.

Why It Works For Trades

Nobody argues with a photo of the finished job.

Before And After Beats Everything

A drain cleared, a switchboard tidied, a dead lawn brought back. It needs no caption and no clever idea. It does what a written testimonial does, faster, and it is impossible to fake convincingly.

Local Reach, Not Follower Counts

Ten thousand followers in another state are worth nothing to you. Two hundred people inside your service area who have seen your work three times are worth a great deal. We optimise for the second thing, which means the numbers look less impressive and the phone rings more.

Profiles That Match The Website

Same logo, same phone number, same service list, same suburbs. Details that disagree across your profiles confuse customers and quietly weaken the local search signals your SEO depends on.

How We Run It

A system you will actually keep up.

It starts with the profiles: Instagram and Facebook set up or cleaned up properly — bio, contact buttons, service area, a working link to the site, and the same details as everywhere else you appear online.

Then a shooting habit that survives a work day. One wide shot before you start. One from the same spot when you finish. Hold the phone still for six seconds instead of waving it around. That is the whole discipline, and it takes less time than writing the invoice.

Then posting: either a routine you can run from the ute in five minutes, or we take the photos you send and handle the editing, captions and schedule. Two posts a week sustained for a year beats daily posting that dies in March. We would genuinely rather you did less and kept going.

What we will talk you out of: buying followers, posting stock photos of somebody else's work, and running a competition to inflate a number that will never call you. A trade account with three hundred real local followers and a year of finished jobs behind it is worth more than one with ten thousand strangers, because the only metric that matters here is whether the person deciding between you and two other quotes has already seen what you do.

Common Questions

Straight answers, no fine print.

Which platform should a tradie be on?

Usually Instagram and Facebook, and usually the same content on both. Instagram is where before-and-after work performs best. Facebook is where the local community groups live, which is still where a lot of trade referrals actually happen. Skip everything else until those two are running properly.

Do I have to be on camera?

No. The work is the content. Plenty of trade accounts do well with nothing but job footage and a caption. If you are comfortable on camera it does help, because people hire people — but it is not a requirement and we will not push you into it.

Does social media actually bring in work for a tradie?

Not the way ads do, and not immediately. What it does is shorten the trust gap. Someone who has already seen three of your finished jobs is a much easier quote to win, and far less likely to be ringing two competitors at the same time.

Can you run the accounts for me?

Yes. You send photos from the job, we handle editing, captions and the posting schedule. It works best as a partnership, because nobody else can photograph your work for you. See the rest of what we offer under digital marketing.

Got a camera roll full of finished jobs?

That is everything we need to start. Tell us your trade and we will show you what a month of it would look like.
