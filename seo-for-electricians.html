Electrician SEO — Australia-wide

SEO for electricians, for both kinds of job.

An electrical business has two very different customers: the one with no power right now, and the one planning a solar install or a rewire. They search differently, they decide differently, and they are won in two different places.

Why Electrician SEO Is Different

Two completely different kinds of customer.

The power is out right now

Switchboard tripped, half the house dark, something smells hot. That person types "emergency electrician" and a suburb and calls one of the first three results. No reading, no comparing. The Google Business Profile wins or loses that job, not the website.

The planned, higher-value work

Solar, battery storage, EV charger installs, full rewires, switchboard upgrades, smoke alarm compliance and commercial maintenance. These people research for days, compare quotes and read pages properly. That demand is won on the website with real service pages, not on the map.

Compliance searches are free money

Smoke alarm compliance, RCD testing, switchboard upgrades to current standards and rental property safety checks are all searched by landlords and agents who have a deadline. Almost nobody writes pages for them, and they convert well because the person has no choice about buying.

The Work

Three things move the needle. The rest is noise.

Google Business Profile

Primary category set to Electrician, a service area matching the suburbs you genuinely cover, true emergency hours, job photos added regularly rather than once, and a steady review habit. Most electrician profiles were set up years ago and never touched since, which is exactly why there is room to beat them.

A page per service

Nobody searches "electrician". They search "switchboard upgrade cost", "EV charger installation", "smoke alarm compliance" and "no power to half the house", each with a location attached. Each service worth having gets a page written the way people actually search for it.

The technical floor

Fast on a phone, tap-to-call above the fold, valid structured data so Google and AI assistants understand you are an electrician covering specific suburbs and services, and a clean sitemap with no duplicate URLs competing against each other.

Straight Talk

What nobody honest will promise you.

Nobody can guarantee the top of the map pack. It reorders based on where the searcher is standing, so ranking in your own suburb does not mean you rank two suburbs over. Anyone selling a guaranteed number one is either guessing or about to do something that gets your profile suspended.

Profile work can move within weeks. Website rankings usually take three to six months. Electrical is less saturated than plumbing in most markets, which tends to make it a shorter road, but we would rather tell you where you actually sit before you spend anything.

If your site is slow on mobile data or buries the phone number, ranking higher just sends more people to a page that will not convert. Fix the electrician website first. It is cheaper and it works faster.

Local SEO For Electricians

Electrician SEO is really local SEO.

Almost every search that matters to an electrical business carries a location. That makes local SEO for electricians far more winnable than general SEO, because you are competing with the handful of sparkies covering the same postcodes rather than the whole country.

The work is unglamorous and it compounds. The right primary category. A service area that reflects reality instead of all of Victoria. Reviews that keep coming. Six to eight suburb pages chosen around where your work actually originates, and a service page for each job type you want more of.

A good electrician SEO agency will also tell you when the answer is no. If you are already booked out, more volume is not the win. Pricing is published on the pricing page, and the general SEO service page covers the work in detail.

Common Questions

Straight answers, no fine print.

How long does SEO take for an electrical business?

Google Business Profile work can show up in the map pack within a few weeks. Website rankings generally take three to six months. Electrical is usually a little less saturated than plumbing, so the timeline tends to be shorter, but anyone promising page one in a month is guessing.

Is SEO worth it for an electrician?

If you want more emergency callouts, the map pack is where that demand goes and the profile work is high value and fast. If you want the better jobs — solar, EV chargers, rewires, commercial maintenance — that is won with service pages on the website and takes longer but pays considerably more per job.

Should I have a page for EV chargers and solar?

Yes, if you genuinely do that work and want more of it. Those searches have clear commercial intent and far fewer electricians have written proper pages for them than for generic electrical work. A real page about EV charger installation in your area will usually outrank a line item on a services list.

Do I need a separate page for every suburb?

No. Six to eight genuinely different pages covering the areas your work actually comes from will outperform thirty near-identical ones, and thirty near-identical ones is how a site gets treated as thin content.

Do you do SEO on an electrician website you did not build?

Yes, as long as we can get proper access to it. We audit it first and tell you honestly whether SEO is worth doing on the site as it stands, or whether the build is the thing holding you back.
